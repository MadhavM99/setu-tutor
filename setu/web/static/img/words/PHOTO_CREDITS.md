# Word photos

Put one photo or picture per word here, named after the English word (lowercase, spaces as hyphens), e.g. `book.jpg`, `clock.webp`.
The app uses a photo when the file exists and falls back to the drawing when it does not. Numbers always stay as dots.

Use only photos you may redistribute (Unsplash/Pexels licence, CC0, public domain, or your own), and list each below.
